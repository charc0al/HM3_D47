PELVIS URINE STREAM
-------------------

The Agent 47 graph entity is:
  FX_E_WaterCoolerPissStream_Sanity
  ID: cafe1ea44a66d812

It uses the full effect runtime:
  content/main/chunk0/fx_bathroom_watercooler_pelvis_stream.entity.json

The four exclusive emitter bands are selected from drunkenness:

  ParticleEmitterBox
  ID: 619f8fed155dab52
  Levels: 1-3 (small)
  Controller: FX_PB_Spray_Blood (9bdbb5952372e54b)

  ParticleEmitterBox_Medium
  ID: d47a000000000003
  Levels: 4-6 (medium)
  Controller: FX_PB_Spray_Blood_Medium (d47a000000000001)

  ParticleEmitterBox_Large
  ID: d47a000000000004
  Levels: 7-9 (large)
  Controller: FX_PB_Spray_Blood_Large (d47a000000000002)

  ParticleEmitterBox_Blackout
  ID: d47a000000000006
  Level: 10 / blackout
  Controller: FX_PB_Spray_Blood_Blackout (d47a000000000005)

On every ParticleEmitterBox* entity:
  m_fParticlesPerSecond     = base particle count/rate (currently 20)
  m_fParticlesPerSecondMax  = maximum particle count/rate (currently 45)
  m_fSpeedMin / m_fSpeedMax = stream speed (currently 3)
  m_fOuterConeAngle         = stream spread (currently 10)
  m_bEnabled                = normally false; gameplay switches exactly one band on.

On each FX_PB_Spray_Blood* controller:
  m_ParticleColorRangeStart / m_ParticleColorRangeEnd = urine colour.
    Current colour: #d6b51e.
  m_fParticleSizeMin / m_fParticleSizeMax = droplet size.
    Small:    30 / 10
    Medium:   75 / 25
    Large:   120 / 40
    Blackout: 150 / 50
  m_fTimeToLiveSecondsMin / Max = particle lifetime.
    Current: 0.25 / 0.30 seconds.
  m_vGravity = arc/drop strength.
    Current Z: -9.8.
  m_fMotionStretchFactor = how strongly particles stretch with motion.
    Current: 1.
  AlphaCurve = opacity over particle lifetime. The small controller explicitly
    overrides it; the other controllers inherit their base effect's curve unless
    an AlphaCurve override is added to them.

Do not change m_bEnabled in a saved patch unless deliberately changing the
selection graph: the gameplay comparators activate one band at a time.


STREAM AND SOUND START DELAYS
-----------------------------

Voluntary bathroom action and involuntary drink-to-bathroom action share:
  Drunk47BathroomStreamBandDispatchDelay
  ID: cafe1ea44a66d824
  Current delay: 3000 ms

This is the delay before the pelvis stream attaches/renders. Its completion
also starts Drunk47BathroomAudioStartDelay:
  ID: cafe1ea44a66d875
  Current delay: 1 ms

The audio event started by that timer is:
  play_sfx_pipe_valve_leak_water_loop_Drunk47
  ID: cafe1ea44a66d871

Legal toilet use has its own independent stream delay:
  Drunk47LegalToiletStreamStartDelay
  ID: cafe1ea44a66d885
  Current delay: 3000 ms

It also feeds Drunk47BathroomAudioStartDelay after its completion. Therefore,
change d824 for voluntary/involuntary only, and d885 for legal toilets only.

Blackout has a separate stream delay:
  Drunk47BlackoutStreamActivateDelay
  ID: cafe1ea44a66d829
  Current delay: 3000 ms

Ground/puddle spawning on the ordinary bathroom route is separately delayed:
  Drunk47BathroomGroundStartDelay (cafe1ea44a66d718): 4000 ms
  Drunk47BathroomGroundFallbackDelay (cafe1ea44a66d719): 250 ms
